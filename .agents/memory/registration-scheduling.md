---
name: Registration scheduling boundaries
description: Product constraints for registration appointments and notifications.
---

Keep registration scheduling, editing and cancellation inside the existing plot booking/collections form, not on a new booking page or dashboard card. Appointment actions must remain independent of collecting money and must not automatically mark a plot sold or registered.

**Why:** The user explicitly requires appointment-only changes without duplicate receipts, zero-value financial entries, approval workflows, buyer reassignment or agreed-price changes.

**How to apply:** Preserve the existing customer/plot context and locked financial agreement. In a combined form, keyboard submission from appointment inputs must not accidentally submit its payment action. Registration completion comes from an explicit existing agreement/inventory action, never from the appointment date passing.
