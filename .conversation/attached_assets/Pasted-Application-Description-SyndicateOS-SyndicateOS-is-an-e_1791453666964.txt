Application Description: SyndicateOS
SyndicateOS is an enterprise-grade Multi-Tenant Business Syndicate & ERP Platform built specifically for multi-partner investment ventures, real-estate developers, builders, liquor syndicates, and infrastructure contractors across Andhra Pradesh (AP) and Telangana.
Core Capabilities & Modules
Syndicate & Multi-Partner Governance
Dynamic Equity & Capital Ledgers: Real-time management of partner share percentages, capital commitments, cash/bank infusions, dynamic equity calls, and profit distributions.
Internal Dispute Prevention: Transparent, role-based audit trails and ledger statements ensuring every rupee contributed or drawn is reconciled among co-investors and managing partners.
Sector-Specific Venture Workflows
Real Estate Open Plotting: Visual plan builders for layout plotting (East, North, West, South, Corner, and Commercial plots), Vastu compliance tagging, rate-per-sq.yard locks, LP/RERA sanction management, and live booking/inventory matrices.
Residential Construction: Floor-by-floor unit matrices (2BHK, 3BHK, Penthouses), amenity sq.ft allocation, civil construction milestones, and RERA registration tracking.
Liquor Vends & Retail Clusters: State excise gazette vend licensing (TGSBCL/APSBL), daily retail counter settlement registers, and partner stock draws.
Turnkey Civil Infrastructure: BoQ measurement books, item-rate and EPC turnkey milestone schedules, retention deposits, and sub-contractor accounts.
Dedicated Banking & Treasury (Project Accounts)
Isolated Project Accounts: Every venture maps directly to its own dedicated banking facilities (RERA Statutory 70% Protected Escrows, Syndicate Capital Pools, Commercial Current Accounts, and Site Petty Cash).
Double-Entry Transaction Flow: Clear tracking of customer booking advances, partner infusions, vendor payouts, and verified voucher uploads.
Multi-Role Access & Operational Portals
Dedicated views for Managing Partners, Auditors / Accountants, Site Engineers, and Field Sales Agents, with print-ready voucher slips, receipts, and ledger statements.
